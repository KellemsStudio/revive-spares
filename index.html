Ordering

How to order

  1. Send us your vehicle details

    The part you need plus your VIN, engine code or licence disc. That's how we match the exact part.

  2. Fata checks the shelf

    He confirms stock and price, then sends photos of the actual part with its stock code.

  3. Pay, then collect or courier

    Pay by EFT or cash at the yard. Couriered parts usually arrive in 2 to 4 working days.
